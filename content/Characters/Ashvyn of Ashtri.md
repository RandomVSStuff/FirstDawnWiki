Ashvyn is a renowned explorer, traveling and mapping the far corners of the world. He comes from an ancient family, said to have descended directly from [[The Godess Ashtri]], passing down knowledge and traditions from times that seem lost to all. He prides himself on telling a good story, but his true self is only shared with those he is close with.
